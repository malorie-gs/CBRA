About CBRA

Purpose

The Criminal Behavior Research Archive is an open research resource for the study of crime, psychology, and human behavior.

CBRA is designed to organize documented cases and make it easier to examine information, identify patterns, and access relevant research.

Methodology

Cases and information in the archive should be supported by reliable sources. Information this is uncertain, disputed, or unproven should be identified as such.

Sources

Case information should be accompanied by references so that users can examine the original sources themselves.
