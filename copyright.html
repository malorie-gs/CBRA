CBRA POLICY

Copyright & Usage

Information about the use of original CBRA content and third-party material appearing throughout the archive.

ORIGINAL CONTENT

CBRA Material

Original material created specifically for the Criminal Behavior Research Archive is protected by applicable copyright law.

Unless otherwise indicated, original content created specifically for CBRA—including original research summaries, descriptions, written analysis, website text, database organization, graphics, and website code—is the property of The Criminal Behavior Research Archive.

Visitors may:

  • View and read publicly available CBRA content for personal research, study, and educational purposes.
  • Cite CBRA as a source in academic, educational, or research work.
  • Link directly to CBRA pages from another website, publication, or research project.
  • Quote limited portions of original CBRA material where permitted by applicable copyright law, with appropriate attribution.

Visitors may not:

  • Reproduce substantial portions of CBRA's original content without permission.
  • Copy and republish CBRA pages or database contents as their own work.
  • Redistribute CBRA's original materials as a separate competing archive without permission.

THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL

Material We Do Not Own

CBRA references material created and published by other individuals and organizations.

CBRA may contain references to material created or published by third parties, including news organizations, authors, photographers, publishers, courts, government agencies, and other organizations.

CBRA does not claim ownership of third-party copyrighted material.

Third-party material remains subject to the rights of its respective copyright owner. Where applicable, CBRA identifies the source, author, publisher, date, and/or original location of such material.

The presence of a source, citation, attribution, or link on CBRA does not by itself mean that CBRA owns or has unrestricted permission to reproduce that material.

COPYRIGHT EXCEPTIONS

Research & Fair Dealing

CBRA is intended to support research and education.

Canadian copyright law provides certain exceptions for purposes including research, private study, education, criticism, review, and news reporting.

Whether a particular use qualifies as fair dealing depends on the circumstances. Merely identifying or crediting a copyright owner does not automatically make a use fair.

Where CBRA relies on an applicable copyright exception, the relevant material should be appropriately identified and attributed.

For information about Canadian copyright law, visitors should consult the Canadian Intellectual Property Office and the Copyright Act.

MEDIA

Images, Documents & Other Media

Media appearing in the archive may have different ownership and usage conditions.

Images, court documents, PDFs, videos, audio, and other media displayed or referenced by CBRA may belong to third parties.

Unless CBRA expressly identifies material as original CBRA material or as being available under a particular licence, visitors should assume that third-party material remains protected by the rights of its respective owner.

Visitors should obtain permission or determine whether an applicable copyright exception or licence permits their intended use before reproducing third-party material.

SOURCES

Linking to Original Sources

CBRA encourages visitors to consult original sources whenever possible.

A link to an external source does not transfer ownership of that source's content to CBRA and does not grant visitors permission to reproduce the linked material.

When available, CBRA provides source information so researchers can locate and consult the original publication or record.

RESEARCH USE

Citing CBRA

CBRA may be cited as a research resource.

Visitors may cite CBRA in academic, educational, or research work.

When citing CBRA, provide enough information for others to identify the relevant case, page, or resource. Where applicable, retain the original source information supplied by CBRA.

COPYRIGHT CONCERNS

Copyright Inquiries

CBRA takes legitimate copyright concerns seriously.

If you believe material appearing on CBRA infringes your copyright or has been incorrectly attributed, please contact CBRA with the following information:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work.
  • Identification and location of the material in question.
  • Your name and contact information.
  • An explanation of your concern.
  • Information demonstrating your ownership or authorization to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

CBRA will review legitimate copyright concerns and may remove, replace, correct, or modify material where appropriate.

RIGHTS

No Transfer of Third-Party Rights

Nothing on this website grants visitors ownership of, or permission to reproduce, third-party copyrighted material merely because that material is referenced, cited, linked, or displayed by CBRA.
